Web Audio Speaker Tones

A minimal, dependency-free demo of generating test tones with the Web Audio API. Everything runs in your browser. No audio is recorded or uploaded.

Safety: start at a low volume. Do not play tones while wearing earbuds or headphones at high volume. Stop if you hear rattling or distortion.
